Is your training program compliance-ready?

A practical 35-point review for teams managing mandatory learning, learner evidence and SCORM content.

How to use it
Work through each control with the program owner. Keep evidence for every completed item—not only a verbal confirmation.

Governance & scope

Make ownership, audience and review rules explicit before content is assigned.

0 / 5 complete

Link each program to the specific law, standard, contract or internal policy that requires it.

Essential

Record who approves content, handles exceptions and signs off on completion reporting.

Essential

Cover employees, contractors, vendors, new joiners and role-specific groups where relevant.

Scope

Define start date, due date, reassignment frequency and treatment for leave or late joiners.

Timing

State who can grant an exemption and what happens after reminders or missed deadlines.

Learning design

Design

Delivery & SCORM

Test launch, tracking and completion behavior before the campaign opens.

0 / 5 complete

Verify the final SCORM ZIP—not only the authoring-tool preview—using a test learner account.

Essential

Confirm completed, passed, failed, score and session duration map correctly into reports.

Essential

SCORM

Test the environments learners actually use, including managed devices and mobile layouts.

QA

Use a pilot group to confirm enrolment, notifications, due dates and escalation recipients.

Privacy & access

Collect only what is needed and control who can see learner-level information.

0 / 5 complete

Include profile fields, country, role, scores, activity timestamps and any custom attributes.

Essential

Align with the privacy team on the applicable basis, notices and employee transparency duties.

Essential

Give report, content and user-management access only to people who require it.

Access

Balance audit evidence requirements against the need to remove data that is no longer required.

Retention

Record processors, sub-processors, hosting regions and relevant contractual protections.

Evidence & reporting

Make the audit trail reproducible without relying on screenshots or memory.

0 / 5 complete

Name the system and report used for final completion numbers and audit responses.

Essential

Keep learner identity, course version, assignment date, completion date, status and score.

Essential

Compare HR or identity data with the LMS population and document exclusions.

Reconcile

Archive the deployed package, reviewer approval and version history for the relevant cycle.

Archive

06

Accessibility & QA

Remove avoidable barriers and provide a documented alternative when needed.

0 / 5 complete

Confirm every control is reachable, focus is visible and the order follows the content.

A11y

Caption meaningful audio accurately and provide text alternatives for audio-first content.

Essential

Review at 200% zoom and avoid text embedded in images without an equivalent.

A11y

Remove unnecessary timers and define an accommodation process where timing is essential.

A11y

Launch & operations

Treat the campaign as an operated process, not a one-time upload.

0 / 5 complete

Include different roles, devices, regions and access needs; record fixes before launch.

Essential

Comms

Schedule completion reviews early enough to resolve access issues before the deadline.
